Doom example for S40 Homebrew

DoomGeneric engine: https://github.com/ozkl/doomgeneric
Base checkout: e257a09527062bfb986b6e5e0780839b6cbd5b71

The engine and native/doom_s40.c backend are GPL-2.0-or-later; see COPYING.DOOM.
The backend implements DoomGeneric's platform interface and includes the Doom
low-memory compatibility data from the engine's i_system.c. The engine's original
copyright and license notices remain in its source checkout. Local engine
modifications remain covered by its license.

The independently written launcher, input mapper and build configuration in
this example are MIT, as is the shared S40 Homebrew foundation; see COPYING.MIT
in the JAR or s40/LICENSE in the source tree. The combined Doom executable
is distributed under GPL-2.0-or-later, with the MIT and third-party notices
retained for their respective components.

The engine source is in this fork's doomgeneric/ directory.
No Doom IWAD is bundled. Original game data is installed separately and is
not covered by the engine's source license.
